💡 Outputs — LEDs, buzzer, relay

        
These pins can switch things on and off. Pin 2 is the little LED on Coggy itself.

        
2 ★45131416171819232526273233

      

      

        
📈 Analog sensors — LDR, knob, soil, gas

        
Read values from 0 to 4095. Pins 34, 35, 36, 39 are input only — perfect for sensors, not for LEDs.

        
323334353639

      

      

        
🖥️ OLED display (I²C)

        
Connect VCC → 3V3, GND → GND, and these two wires:

        
SDA → 21SCL → 22

      

      

        
👆 Touch pins

        
Touch a wire on these pins and the value drops.

        
4131415273233

      

      

        
⛔ Avoid these

        
6–11 are used by Coggy's memory. 1 and 3 talk to the USB cable. 0, 12, 15 can stop Coggy from starting if wired wrong.

Welcome to Block Studio!

      
Snap colourful blocks together and bring your Coggy to life — LEDs, sensors and an OLED screen.

    

    

      

        
1
🔌
Plug in Coggy
Connect Coggy with a USB cable, then click Connect Coggy.

        
2
🧩
Snap blocks
Drag blocks into the Coggy Program. Type the pin number where you wired your part.

        
3
🚀
Upload!
Click Upload and watch your LEDs, sensors and OLED come alive.

      

      

        
        
      

      
🛠️ First time with this Coggy? It needs MicroPython firmware (a one-time setup by your teacher). Block Studio installs its own helper library automatically on the first upload.
